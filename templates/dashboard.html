How to Interpret This Option Chain to Take High-Probability Trades
🔴 Red Call OI (Resistance): Option writers sell Calls when betting price won't cross that strike. When spot touches the highest Call OI strike with negative CVD, it signals an ideal Put Buying (PE) or short-sell reversal!

📊 PCR & Max Pain: PCR > 1.2: Put writers dominate (Bullish dip-buying mode). PCR < 0.8: Call writers dominate (Bearish rally-selling mode). Max Pain: Expiry magnet where option sellers make maximum profit.

Learn Open Interest & Option Chain on the Spot (Trader's Handbook)

Everything you need to interpret the chart and take high-probability trades

1. Who is in Control: Red (Calls) vs Green (Puts)

In derivatives, institutions are option sellers (writers), because they have the margin capital. Retailers are mostly buyers.

  • 🔴 Red Bars (Call OI): Institutions sell Calls when they believe the market will NOT go above that strike. High Call OI acts as an overhead Ceiling / Resistance.
  • 🟢 Green Bars (Put OI): Institutions sell Puts when they believe the market will NOT drop below that strike. High Put OI creates a cushioned Floor / Support.
  • ⚡ Rule of Thumb: If Green bars tower over Red bars, big players are betting on upmoves. If Red bars dominate, big players are capping the rally.
2. How the Algo Interprets This to Take Trades
  • 🎯 Call Buying Setup (CE): When spot pulls back to the highest Put OI strike (e.g. 23,650) and bounces with positive CVD delta. Put writers defend this strike, providing a safe stop-loss.
  • 🎯 Put Buying Setup (PE): When spot rallies into the highest Call OI strike (e.g. 23,700) and gets rejected with negative CVD delta. Call writers defend this ceiling.
  • 🚀 Short-Covering Explosion: If price breaks above the highest Call OI, Call writers panic and buy back their positions, causing an explosive upward gamma surge!
3. Option Matrix Column Definitions (Layman's Quick Reference)
LTP (₹) Last Traded Price. Total contract cost = LTP × Lot Size (65).
STRIKE & ATM Contract target price. ATM is the strike nearest to live index spot.
OI (Open Interest) Total active contracts held open overnight. Represents institutional money.
VOLUME Total contracts traded today. High volume + rising OI = high conviction.
DELTA 0.50 means option gains ₹5 for every 10 pt move in Nifty spot.
IV% (Implied Vol) Market expectation of swing size. High IV = expensive options; Low IV = cheap.
